Support the Work

Transparent, direct, and 100% community-focused. Every shilling stays in Turkana.

What Your Support Achieves

We believe in radical transparency. Here is exactly what your contribution provides:

KES 1,000

Protect a Worker

Provides a full set of PPE (heavy gloves, gumboots, safety goggles, and N95 mask) for one clearing crew member for a month.

KES 5,000

Fuel the Transformation

Covers the fuel and binder materials needed to produce 50kg of smokeless eco-briquettes or safe livestock feed.

KES 25,000

Equip the Hub

Contributes directly to the purchase and maintenance of a manual briquette press or hammer mill for the community cooperative.
