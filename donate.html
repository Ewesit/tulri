Where Your Money Goes

We believe in total transparency. Here is exactly how your contribution directly impacts Turkana West:

KSh 500

Provides basic clearing tools (gloves, slashers) for one community volunteer.

KSh 2,000

Funds the reseeding of indigenous grasses on 1 acre of cleared land.

KSh 10,000

Trains a women's group in eco-briquette production for a month.
