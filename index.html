Where We Work

TULRI targets Prosopis clearing in four critical zones that directly impact community survival, safety, and prosperity.

🐄

Grazing Lands

Restoring native grasslands like Natamakarwo to secure pasture for cattle, goats, sheep, and camels.

💧

Water Points

Clearing access to the Tarach River, wells, and seasonal water sources for livestock and communities.

🛣️

Roads & Highways

Removing dangerous thickets that block visibility, cause accidents, and provide cover for criminals.

🌾

Farms & Croplands

Protecting agro-pastoralist farms from Prosopis invasion that threatens food security and livelihoods.

From Threat to Treasure: Our Circular Bioeconomy

We don't just clear Prosopis—we transform it into community wealth. Every cleared tree becomes a resource for livelihoods, fencing, and sustainable energy.

1. Clear

Uproot Prosopis from critical grazing lands, water points, roads, and farms.

→

2. Transform

Convert wood into eco-briquettes, poles, and biochar

→

3. Generate Income

Sell products to fund restoration & support households

🌍 Environmental Impact

  • ✓ No open burning — Zero air pollution, no wildfire risk
  • ✓ Seed destruction — Heat treatment prevents re-spread
  • ✓ Native tree protection — Prosopis poles replace acacia fencing
  • ✓ Soil improvement — Biochar enhances moisture retention
