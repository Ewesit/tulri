Our Work

Turning an ecological threat into community wealth, safety, and lasting peace.

A Holistic, Community-Led Approach

We don't just cut down trees. TULRI employs a comprehensive, evidence-based model that addresses the root causes of land degradation while creating immediate economic and security benefits for the community.

🌳

Land Restoration & Grazing

Clearing invasive Prosopis from critical zones like Natamakarwo and the Tarach River, followed by reseeding indigenous grasses to restore pastoralist livelihoods.

Sustainable Livelihoods
