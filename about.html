About TULRI

Restoring Land, Securing Livelihoods, and Rebuilding Peace in Turkana West.

Our Story

The Turkana Livelihoods & Restoration Initiative (TULRI) was born out of an urgent reality: the unchecked invasion of Prosopis juliflora (Etirae) is choking our rivers, blocking our roads, and threatening our way of life.

What started as grassroots clearing efforts along the Tarach River and the fertile plains of Natamakarwo has grown into a comprehensive restoration initiative. We realized that to truly solve this crisis, we couldn't just cut down trees—we had to create sustainable livelihoods and rebuild the peace fractured by shrinking resources.

Our Mission

To empower communities to reclaim degraded lands from Prosopis invasion through participatory restoration, sustainable livelihoods, and cross-border environmental peacebuilding.

Our Vision

A resilient, peaceful, and ecologically restored Turkana where shared natural resources foster cross-border cohesion rather than conflict.

Our Core Values

Community Ownership Led by local people.
Ecological Justice Restoring nature's balance.
Eco-Peacebuilding Uniting communities.
Resilience Adapting to climate change.
Inclusion Women and youth first.

Where We Work

We target Prosopis clearing in four critical zones that directly impact community survival and safety.

🐄

Grazing Lands

Restoring native grasslands like Natamakarwo to secure pasture and prevent cross-border resource conflicts.

💧

Water Points

Clearing access to the Tarach River and wells, reducing the time burden on women and children.

🛣️

Roads & Highways

Removing dangerous thickets that block visibility, cause accidents, and hide criminals.

🌾

Farms & Croplands

Protecting agro-pastoralist farms from invasion to safeguard local food security.

"We are not just clearing a weed. We are clearing the path for our children to graze their cattle safely, and for our youth to build a future instead of fighting over dry land."
— Community Elder, Turkana West
