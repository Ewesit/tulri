Roles Within Our Community

TULRI is building a self-sustaining local economy. Here is how community members directly participate:

🌿 Clearing Crew Member

Trained locals who safely uproot Prosopis using proper PPE and tools, earning daily stipends.

⚙️ Processing Cooperative Member

Women and youth trained to operate briquette presses and hammer mills, turning waste into income.

🛒 Local Customer

Households and schools that buy our smokeless eco-briquettes, keeping the revenue within Turkana.

👑 Grazing Committee Elder

Community leaders who guide our targeting strategy, ensuring we clear the most critical corridors first.
